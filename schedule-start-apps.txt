
Register/update the Windows scheduled task:

powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "C:\Repo\stocks_au_web\scripts\register_pegasus_scheduler.ps1"

Default registered task:
\pegasus\Pegasus Apps

The task action starts the whole app:
powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "C:\Repo\stocks_au_web\start-apps.ps1" -BackendPort 3101 -FrontendPort 3100 -LogPath "C:\Repo\stocks_au_web\logs" -NoNewWindows
