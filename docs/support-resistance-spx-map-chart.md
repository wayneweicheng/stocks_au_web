# SPX Map levels in Support / Resistance

In **Stock Map Levels**, enter `SPX`, `ESMain`, `SPY_D`, `QQQ_D`, `SPXL_D`, `TQQQ_D`, `SOXL_D` or `SOXX_D`. These labels load completed **SPX Map** JSON reports; an ordinary ticker such as `SPY` still loads its own **Stock Map** reports. The dropdown never mixes the two report types.

- `SPX` plots the report's `primary_supports`/`primary_resistances` **SPX `zone_low`/`zone_high`**, reference anchor, score, confidence and SPXW option-node evidence against `SPXW.US` candles. ES values printed on the report are not plotted on the SPX axis.
- `ESMain` adds the SPX Map page's **same-date cash-close ES−SPX basis** to each SPX zone and anchor; the chart uses `ESMain.US` candles. If the SPX/ES cash closes have different dates, no derived levels are plotted.
- `SPY_D` uses the SPX Map page's SPX-percent-to-SPY-close translation; `QQQ_D` uses its observed SPY/QQQ IB move ratio or difference; `SPXL_D` maps the SPY move ×3; `TQQQ_D` maps the QQQ move ×3. `SOXL_D` and `SOXX_D` each map independently through the observed SPY/target IB move (ratio for same-direction moves above 0.20%, otherwise target-minus-SPY move spread). They are **not** interchangeable semiconductor indices, so SOXL is not assumed to be SOXX ×3. Candles come from each label's unsuffixed `.US` underlying. Same-date historical closes are required; QQQ, TQQQ, SOXL and SOXX additionally require positive quote-only SPY and corresponding target IB prices. Semiconductor quotes are used only when the SPX close date is the latest eligible date. Missing inputs leave the chart candles visible but **no fabricated derived levels or raw SPX prices**.
- Each derived card and chart hover shows the target's mapped zone, **mapped reference anchor with signed percentage versus its own selected 30-minute close (and that close's date)**, and its source SPX anchor and SPX zone. Scores, confidence and option details are from the SPX report; they do not measure the target's own options. Derived support/resistance sides remain those of the source SPX map because cash closes or live calibrations used for projection can postdate the report as-of. Derived ranges are display-time translations, not immutable report levels or trading instructions. No range, futures or put-order links are offered for any SPX-sourced level.
- The SPX-only ATM card reads `StockDB_US.StockData.v_OptionDelayedQuote_V2` for **SPXW** with `ObservationDate = the selected SPX report's US as-of date`. It selects a positive same-strike, same-expiry call/put near the report SPX reference; bid/ask mid takes precedence over valid last. An absent pair remains absent—**no prior-day fallback**. `ObservationDate` alone does not prove quote availability at map as-of or indicate a live quote. The displayed premium band concerns the actual expiry, not an intraday or next-session prediction. Other derived symbols do not inherit a fictional SPXW ATM quote for their own underlying.

All report lists, JSON artifacts, price snapshots, chart bars and option rows are read-only. No jobs or orders are submitted by this view.
